Sabakk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Developer: techTMB
Privacy contact: techtmbmalta@gmail.com
App: Sabakk (Android package com.techtmb.sabakk)

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the current offline release of Sabakk, this privacy-policy website, and privacy/support enquiries. You do not need an account or an internet connection to play. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

2. Data on your device

Sabakk stores your saved match, game history, cards, chip totals, and setup preferences in the app’s private storage so you can resume play. Preferences include opponent count, difficulty, starting chips, and token choices. This data is processed on your device; the offline app does not upload gameplay or preferences to us.

The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifiers. It does not ask for your real name, email, payment details, or date of birth to play. Chips have no monetary value and the app does not process payments.

3. Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up your saved match and setup preferences, or transfer them to another device, depending on your device and backup settings. Your backup provider, such as Google or your device manufacturer, handles that service under its own privacy terms. We do not receive access to your backup. You can manage backup and transfer settings through Android and your backup provider.

4. Policy website, external links, and support

Opening this policy online or a source link launches your browser. The website host receives information normally sent with a web request, including IP address, browser information, request time, and possibly a referring URL. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, forms, external fonts, or tracking pixels to this page. The host may process technical access data for delivery and security under its own policy.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your enquiry, investigate an issue, or respond to a privacy request. Our email providers process these communications to deliver and store them. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. When data is shared

The offline app does not send your saved games or preferences to us or other players. Your chosen backup provider may handle backups as described above. Website hosting providers process web-request data when you open a page, and email providers process messages when you contact us. Third-party websites linked from the app have their own privacy practices.

We may disclose information you send us if required by law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences remain until replaced or removed. To delete the app’s local data, use Android Settings > Apps > Sabakk > Storage > Clear storage (labels vary by device), or uninstall the app. Cloud backups can outlast removal from your device or be restored later; manage or delete those through your backup provider’s settings.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the enquiry, follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the privacy contact above. Website and email providers may retain technical/security records under their own retention practices. Deleting the app does not automatically delete those separate records.

We cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device or directly delete backups controlled by your backup provider. There is no Sabakk user account to delete.

7. Security and your choices

Local files use Android’s app-private storage. The public privacy-policy page uses HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security. You can manage Android backups, clear local app data, and choose whether to open external links or contact us.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, obtain a copy, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us using the address above. We will respond as required by applicable law and may need enough information to identify your request. Hosting and email services may process data in countries other than your own; their published policies describe their locations and transfer safeguards.

8. Children’s privacy

Playing Sabakk does not require personal information or an age profile. We do not use behavioural advertising or knowingly request children’s personal information to play. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can investigate and address data we control.

9. Changes and provider policies

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Before releasing features that change how personal data is handled, we will update the relevant disclosures.
